Privacy Policy for Coin Drop

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Coin Drop is a casual arcade game developed by COIN DROP DEVELOPERS.

We respect your privacy. Coin Drop is designed to provide a simple gameplay experience without requiring you to create an account or provide personal information.

Information We Collect

Coin Drop does not collect, request, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, or other personally identifiable information.

The game does not require you to create an account or log in.

Game Data

Coin Drop may store game-related information, such as your score, high score, lives, or other gameplay progress, locally on your device so that this information can be displayed within the game.

This locally stored game information is not transmitted to COIN DROP DEVELOPERS.

Internet and Data Sharing

Coin Drop is designed to work without an internet connection.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

The game does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

Permissions

Coin Drop does not require access to sensitive device permissions for its core gameplay.

Data Security

Because Coin Drop does not collect or transmit personal information, no personal information is stored on our servers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Coin Drop does not maintain a personal user database or store personal information on our servers.

Game-related information stored locally on your device can generally be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

Coin Drop does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users because the game does not collect personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the features or data practices of Coin Drop change. Any updated version will include a new "Last updated" date.
